Steamworks library (libsteam_api.so, libsteam_api.dylib, steam_api64.dll)

These files are made by Valve Corporation. They are the Steamworks redistributable
library, and Valve publishes them for other programs to ship. They are not covered by
the licence of RimWorld Profile Manager.

They are used by one program only: rwpm-steam. It asks the Steam client that is already
running to download, subscribe to or unsubscribe from RimWorld Workshop mods, when you
restore a modpack and choose that. The main program, rwpm, never loads them.

Valve's terms for the library are in its Steamworks SDK Access Agreement.
